## Decks

### Zápisky z bojového pole — 20 let se SharePointem

**[Download PDF](/talks/zapisky-z-bojoveho-pole-20-let-se-sharepointem.pdf ':ignore :target=_blank')** · 22 slides · Czech
📍 [WUG Days 2026](https://www.wug.cz/brno/akce/1875-WUG-Days-2026), 2–3 September 2026, Brno

*Notes from the field — 20 years with SharePoint, and what I would do differently today.*

Two decades of SharePoint work told as war stories rather than theory, each one structured the way this vault's gotchas are: **symptom → cause → lesson**. Five chapters — the myths that persist, where permissions are lost, the chaos we create for ourselves, security without varnish, and operations and upgrades.

The myths chapter alone is worth the read if you have ever heard "we will buy it and it will organise itself" or "one IT person can handle the whole thing".

### SharePoint jako znalostní platforma pro firemní AI

**[Download PDF](/talks/sharepoint-jako-znalostni-platforma-pro-firemni-ai.pdf ':ignore :target=_blank')** · 25 slides · Czech
📍 [WUG Days 2026](https://www.wug.cz/brno/akce/1875-WUG-Days-2026), 2–3 September 2026, Brno

*SharePoint as the knowledge platform for enterprise AI — what to do before you buy Copilot licences, and why it need not be Copilot at all.*

The argument in one line: **AI is only as good as the SharePoint underneath it.** Copilot does not improve your content, it amplifies its current state — good data gives great answers, chaos gives confidently presented chaos. Roughly 80 % of the outcome is decided before the first licence is bought.

Covers where knowledge actually lives, the five pillars of preparation, the limits of M365 Copilot, licensing alternatives, and the governance that starts rather than ends at go-live. Closes with a concrete Monday-morning step: run a permissions scan and pick one library for a pilot knowledge base.
